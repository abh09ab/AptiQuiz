## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Push Code to Your GitHub Repository
Run this one command in your terminal inside the project root:
```bash
git push -u origin main
```
This uploads all modules (`Module_1_Structure` through `Module_7_Milestone_Submission_Kit`) directly to your active repository: `https://github.com/abh09ab/AptiQuiz`.

---

### Step 2: Unlock Dashboard with Prerequisite Onboarding
1. Go to **Offer Letter** tab on your left sidebar in the dashboard.
2. Download your PDF offer letter.
3. Open LinkedIn and create a post using the **Prerequisite Onboarding** template. Tag Codomax Digital.
4. Copy your LinkedIn post link.
5. In the dashboard on the **Prerequisite Onboarding** card:
   - Click the input field under **Submission Deliverables** -> **LinkedIn Post Link**.
   - Paste your LinkedIn post URL.
   - Click **Submit Link**.
6. This immediately unlocks Module 1!

---

### Step 3: Submitting Modules 1 through 4
For each card:
1. Open LinkedIn and post the corresponding update from `LinkedIn_Posts_Templates.md`.
2. Copy the post link from LinkedIn.
3. On the module card in your Codomax dashboard:
   - Paste the **GitHub Repository Link** (from the table above).
   - Paste the **LinkedIn Post Link**.
   - Click **Submit**.

---

### Step 4: Submitting Module 5 & 6 (Deployment & Final)
1. In **Module 5**:
   - Paste the GitHub link: `https://github.com/abh09ab/AptiQuiz/tree/main/Codomax_Portfolio/Module_5_Contact_Deployment`
   - Paste your Live Website Link (GitHub Pages or Vercel URL).
   - Paste your Module 5 LinkedIn Post Link.
   - Click Submit.
2. In **Module 6**:
   - Paste the GitHub link: `https://github.com/abh09ab/AptiQuiz/tree/main/Codomax_Portfolio/Module_6_Final_Portfolio`
   - Paste your Module 6 LinkedIn Post Link.
   - Click Submit.

---

### Step 5: Claiming Graduation Milestone & Certificate
Once all deliverables are approved:
1. The **Graduation Milestone** card unlocks.
2. The **Graduation Tracker** on the bottom left flips from `0% Complete` to `100% Complete`.
3. Go to the **Certificate + LOR** tab on the left sidebar to download:
   - 📜 **Official MSME Govt. Verified Certificate**
   - 🆔 **Lifetime HR Verification ID**
   - 📑 **Bonus ATS Resume Templates**
4. Share the Graduation LinkedIn post to celebrate your achievement!
